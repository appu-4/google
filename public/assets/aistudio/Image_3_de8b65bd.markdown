## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an **Architectural Eco-Modernism** philosophy tailored for high-trust industrial circularity. The visual language marries the rigorous precision of sustainable architectural blueprints with the editorial tactile warmth of natural, raw materials.

### Brand Narrative & Identity
- **Personality:** Authoritative, ecologically grounded, precise, regenerative, and intellectually luminous.
- **Target Audience:** Commercial architects, salvage contractors, sustainability officers, deconstruction engineers, and corporate real estate developers sourcing verified structural and interior materials.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute confidence in technical viability, material provenance, and ecological impact, avoiding greenwashing cliches in favor of industrial-grade transparency.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Tactile Structuralism:** Interfaces mimic structural architectural boards and physical material swatches through crisp, hair-thin boundary lines, measured proportions, and restrained, tactile surfaces.
- **Data-Driven Transparency:** Quantifiable ecological data (embodied carbon offset, moisture grades, structural ratings) are elevated to primary visual anchors alongside high-fidelity salvage imagery.
- **Warm Restraint:** High contrast without eye fatigue. Pristine alabaster fields meet profound forest greens and baked clay accents, providing immediate semantic legibility.

## Colors

The palette establishes an architectural dialogue between raw structural matter, verified digital intelligence, and environmental stewardship.

### Primary Role: Forest Pine (`#1E4D3B`)
- Represents endurance, structural longevity, and natural stewardship.
- Used for high-priority interactive triggers, core navigation anchors, primary button fills, and dominant brand headers.
- **Hover/Active State:** `#163A2C` ensures a deep, grounded response under cursor interaction.

### Secondary Role: Terracotta Clay (`#D96B27`)
- Represents raw earth, brick salvage, reclamation urgency, and dynamic auction/reserve actions.
- Used deliberately for material recovery alerts, rapid-depletion stock indicators, lot close timers, and secondary interactive calls to action.

### Tertiary Role: Teal Slate (`#0F766E`)
- Denotes AI intelligence, structural verification algorithms, automated spec matching, and circular lifecycle ratings.
- Paired with its soft-tint container `#F0FDFA` for data chips, AI scan badges, and confidence metrics.

### Neutral Roles & Surfaces
- **Canvas / App Background:** Warm Alabaster (`#F9F8F6`), evoking unbleached architectural sketch paper and natural limestone.
- **Surface / Card Background:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), offering sharp separation from the alabaster floor.
- **Structural Outlines:** Neutral Stone Border (`#E5E2DC`), standard 1px framing for all surfaces, table cells, and dividers.
- **Primary Text:** Charcoal Slate (`#18181B`), meeting WCAG AAA contrast for technical specifications.
- **Secondary Text:** Muted Slate (`#52525B`), applied to secondary metadata, field labels, and supplementary logs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy operates across three specialized font families, mapping purpose cleanly to form:

1. **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean contemporary geometry with humanist warmth. Used across major page sections, material taxonomy headers, and modal titles.
2. **Interface Body (`Inter`):** Handles general user interface text, procedural descriptions, inspection reports, and form configurations with neutral, utilitarian clarity.
